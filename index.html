              About this map & analysis notes
            
            

              Paths show reachability in the two locked trees, not why npm
              selected a version. Each change is matched by installed location.
              Moved packages appear as removed and added. Signals are prompts
              for review, not vulnerability findings. Linked workspaces are
              outside the path model.
